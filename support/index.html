What to include

Describe what you tried, what happened and what you expected. Include your device model, Android or iOS version, CometDue version if known and any error message. Redact private information from screenshots.

For purchase access, include the store, approximate purchase date and relevant order or transaction reference if available. Never send passwords, verification codes, bank logins or full card details. Do not post sensitive information in public GitHub issues.

Purchase access and refunds

If Pro is missing after a completed purchase, connect to the internet and use Restore Purchases in Settings or on the Pro screen with the same store account. Pending purchases need store confirmation. Purchases do not automatically transfer between the App Store and Google Play.

Use the official Apple or Google route for billing refunds, or email us for access help and developer-handled Google Play requests. Purchases & Refunds provides the details.

Records and backups

We do not have a server copy of your bills, savings or game history. Restore Purchases restores premium access only. Recover records using an available CometDue backup or device backup. Recovery cannot be guaranteed without a usable backup.

Manual backups are readable JSON containing financial information. If an investigation needs a file, consider whether a redacted screenshot or sample will suffice. Share a complete backup only when necessary and you choose to disclose it. Importing replaces current local records after confirmation.

Reminders

Check notification permission, the app's reminder settings, device timezone, and battery or background restrictions. Open CometDue periodically to refresh upcoming reminders. Operating-system limits can still delay or prevent delivery.

Notifications can show bill names and amounts. Adjust lock-screen previews if you want to hide them, and keep another way to check important deadlines.

Privacy and deletion

Use Settings > Delete local data for this installation's records. For correspondence or purchase-management data held outside the device, email us with your request. No app installation is needed. Data & Deletion explains what the reset removes, what remains and how to manage other copies.
