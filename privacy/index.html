Who is responsible

CometDue is published by Lloyd Alemania, trading as Sensei IT Solutions, a sole proprietorship based in the Philippines. In this policy, 'we', 'us' and 'our' refer to the publisher. This policy covers the CometDue app, our public legal and support pages, and support correspondence. For privacy questions or requests, email breaksonfridays@gmail.com.

CometDue is an offline-first bill tracker with no CometDue sign-up, account or financial-data server. Your bill and savings records are stored on your device. Purchases, device backups, files you choose to share and messages you send to support involve the separate services described below.

Records stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter: bill names and descriptions, categories, amounts, currencies, due dates, recurrence and reminder settings, recorded payment amounts and dates, payment notes, savings goals, contributions and notes. It also stores your planets, appearance choices, game history, streaks, achievements and preferences.

These records let the app display bills, calculate balances and forecasts, schedule local reminders and provide the game experience. CometDue does not upload these financial or game records to us or RevenueCat during ordinary use. We receive them only if you deliberately send them to us, for example in a support email or attachment.

CometDue does not connect to your bank, read bank accounts or pay bills. Marking a bill as paid records your entry; it does not transfer money. Do not put passwords, bank sign-in details or full payment-card details in notes or support messages.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple or Google processes payments for CometDue Pro Lifetime. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases, determine Pro access, restore purchases, recognize refunds or revocations, and view purchase reporting. RevenueCat's purchase-management service starts when the app opens and can contact its servers even before you make a purchase.

RevenueCat uses a randomly generated app user ID rather than a CometDue account. It processes purchase history and related records, such as product and transaction identifiers, purchase receipts or tokens, purchase status and dates, and technical context needed to operate its service, such as platform, app and operating-system versions, locale and currency. A random ID is still a purchase identifier; it does not mean no data is processed.

We do not provide bill or savings records to RevenueCat. The app does not send a name, email address or advertising identifier through RevenueCat customer attributes. We do not receive your full payment-card details or your Apple or Google account password. A separate local cache keeps the last verified Pro status and verification time so paid access can remain available when the store cannot be reached.

Reminders and device permissions

Reminders are scheduled on your device, rather than sent by a CometDue push-notification server. The app reads the device timezone to schedule them and uses operating-system background work to refresh upcoming reminders. You control reminder categories and timing in the app and notification permission in device settings.

Notifications can show a bill's name, amount and due or overdue status on the lock screen, in notification history or on connected devices, depending on your settings. Adjust notification previews if you want to hide that information. File access and sharing are used when you choose a backup to import or an export destination.

Backups, exports and migration

Backup & Restore creates a readable JSON file containing financial records, game history and selected preferences. CometDue does not add a password or application-level encryption to this file. You choose whether to save or share it through Files, email, a cloud drive or another app. The destination handles its copy under its own rules. Pro ownership is not included in a manual backup.

CometDue permits normal operating-system backup and restore. Depending on your device and settings, app data may be included in Apple, Google, computer or device-to-device backups and transfers. These are controlled by you and the operating system or backup provider. They are not a CometDue cloud-sync service and are not guaranteed to contain the latest changes.

Deleting local data does not delete external exports or existing device backups. Restoring an older backup may bring back information deleted from the current installation. Protect backup files and remove unwanted copies from their destinations separately.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, any name you provide, and any attachments or purchase references you include. We use them to answer your request, investigate issues, verify a purchase or privacy request when necessary, and maintain a record of the resolution. Our email provider processes messages to deliver and store correspondence.

Send only information needed for the request. Redact unrelated details from screenshots or receipts. Do not send your full bill backup unless necessary and you choose to share its contents. We do not use support correspondence to send advertising.

Our public website

Our public legal and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in. GitHub handles hosting data under its privacy statement.

We do not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts or nonessential cookies to these legal pages. External links and email links open services with their own privacy practices. Do not place private bill data or purchase receipts in a public GitHub issue or repository.

Sharing and advertising

We do not sell personal information, display third-party ads or share data for cross-context behavioral advertising. CometDue has no separate behavioral analytics SDK or advertising identifier integration. RevenueCat purchase reporting is used for purchase administration and understanding sales, not to track bills or activity across other apps.

Service providers processing personal data on our behalf must protect it consistently with this policy and applicable law and use it to provide their services to us. Apple, Google, GitHub and destinations you choose for backups also process data for their own services under their policies. We may disclose information we hold when required by law, to respond to a lawful request or when reasonably necessary to protect legal rights, security or people. We limit disclosure to what is appropriate for that purpose.

Purposes and legal grounds

Where a legal basis is required, we process information as necessary to provide the app and purchased features, respond to requests and fulfill our agreement with you; for legitimate interests in operating a secure app, administering purchases, understanding sales and resolving support issues; and to meet applicable legal obligations. Where applicable law requires consent, we rely on that consent and allow its withdrawal.

Revoke notification permission through device settings. Contact us about other consent-based processing or objections. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawful before withdrawal or processing with another lawful basis. Forecasts and game outcomes are calculated from your entries; we do not use them for lending, credit or other decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until removed or you use Settings > Delete local data. Ordinary bill deletion can preserve payment and historical records; it is not a complete reset. The full local deletion action clears financial and game records and preferences, cancels CometDue notifications, and removes backup exports and import copies managed inside the app. It returns the app to its initial setup.

A local reset does not remove store purchase ownership or the separate local Pro entitlement cache. It does not delete Apple, Google or RevenueCat purchase records, support correspondence, external exports or device backups. These require separate action.

We keep support correspondence and purchase-related records accessible to us only as long as needed for the relevant service, follow-up, purchase restoration, security, legal compliance or resolution of disputes. Providers may retain records independently required for their own legal obligations. On a deletion request, we assess the records we hold, remove those no longer needed and explain any applicable retention reason. There is no server copy of your local bills for us to retrieve or erase remotely.

Security and international processing

Local records rely on the operating system's app-storage and device-security protections. CometDue does not add a separate database password or its own encryption layer to financial records or JSON exports. RevenueCat encrypts its service data in transit. No storage or transmission method guarantees complete security. Use a device passcode, keep your device updated and protect backup destinations.

We are based in the Philippines. Providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate, which may have different privacy laws. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we use the required safeguards, including appropriate contractual protections. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your request.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to be informed, access information, correct it, receive a portable copy, request deletion, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent and complain to a privacy regulator. These may include rights under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, the EU or UK GDPR and other regional laws. We do not discriminate against you for exercising applicable rights.

View and edit local records in the app, export them using Backup & Restore, and erase the installation's records using Delete local data. For support messages or purchase data we can access, email breaksonfridays@gmail.com. You can make a request without installing the app. Explain your request and provide the information needed to locate a record, such as the address used for support or a relevant store transaction reference. We may ask for proportionate verification, but never your password or full card details.

We respond within the period required by the law applicable to your request. If a legal exception applies, we explain it. You may complain to the Philippine National Privacy Commission or your local data-protection authority without first contacting us.

Children and young users

CometDue is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you are below the age at which you can independently agree to terms or authorize relevant processing in your country, use the app only with the permission and involvement of a parent or legal guardian. Purchases also require authorization under your store account and local law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through support or purchase management. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required. A parent or guardian may contact us about their child's information.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as the app, services or applicable requirements change. The updated date identifies this version. We provide additional notice and obtain consent before new processing where required by law. Email breaksonfridays@gmail.com if you need help understanding a change or locating an earlier policy.
