No CometDue account to close

CometDue has no user registration or account. Bills, payment entries, savings and game records stay in the app on your device, with possible backup copies you control. Purchase-management records, store accounts and support emails are separate.

Delete this installation's records

Open CometDue > Settings > Delete local data and confirm Delete data. Save a backup first only if you want to retain a copy. The reset cannot be undone from within the app and returns you to initial setup.

The action removes local bills, recorded payments, savings, planets, game history and preferences. It cancels CometDue notifications and removes backup exports and cached import copies managed inside the app. Ordinary bill deletion can preserve payment and historical records; use the full reset to remove the installation's financial history.

Removing app records does not guarantee forensic erasure from physical storage. Your operating system controls underlying storage and device-security features.

What the reset does not delete

Pro purchase ownership and the separate local cache of verified Pro status are preserved. The reset does not cancel or refund the purchase. Eligible Pro access can still be restored with the same store account.

The reset cannot remove records held elsewhere. Manage these separately:

  • Files saved or shared outside CometDue, including copies in Files, email, cloud drives and other apps.
  • Existing computer, device and cloud backups or device-transfer copies.
  • Purchase and transaction records held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat.
  • Support emails and attachments sent to Sensei IT Solutions.

Exports and device backups

Delete exported JSON files from every destination where you saved or shared them, including trash or recently deleted folders where applicable. If you sent a file to someone else, ask that recipient to remove their copy.

Use your operating system or backup provider to manage app backups. CometDue permits normal device backups but cannot remotely erase them. Availability, retention and deletion depend on the provider and your settings. Restoring an older manual or device backup can restore previously deleted information.

Request deletion without the app

Email breaksonfridays@gmail.com with the subject 'CometDue data deletion'. You do not need to install the app or create an account. Explain whether the request concerns support correspondence, RevenueCat purchase-management data or both.

For correspondence, use your original email address or provide enough information to find the conversation. For purchase records, include the platform and relevant store order or transaction reference if available. We may request the minimum information needed to locate a record and verify your authority. Never send passwords, authentication codes or full card details.

We cannot erase the local database on a device we cannot access. If the app is unavailable, use operating-system app-storage or uninstall controls to remove the installation, and manage external backups separately. On iOS, offloading can preserve app data, so choose the device option that deletes both the app and its data.

Purchase records and retained information

For RevenueCat records we control, we assess requests and delete relevant customer data where required and technically available. Deleting a RevenueCat customer record does not refund a purchase or erase Apple or Google transaction history. A later purchase or restore check may create a purchase-management record again to validate access.

Apple and Google manage their own store-account and transaction data. Contact the store about information it controls. Records may need to remain for legal obligations, fraud prevention, purchase restoration or resolving disputes. We explain applicable retention reasons when responding.

We respond within the period required by applicable law. If a request cannot be fulfilled in whole or part, we explain why and available next steps. The Privacy Policy describes your rights and regulator contacts.
