What you purchase

CometDue Pro Lifetime is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase, not a subscription. There is no recurring renewal charge or subscription to cancel. Review the localized store price before confirming. Apple or Google processes payment; RevenueCat manages verification and Pro access.

The free tracker remains available without a purchase. Store terms and applicable consumer law govern transactions. This policy neither promises a refund in every situation nor removes a mandatory right to a remedy.

Apple App Store purchases

For a purchase billed by Apple, use Report a Problem while signed into the Apple Account used for the purchase. Choose the refund option and follow the instructions for that transaction. Apple's support article explains missing or pending charges.

Apple reviews requests under its policies and applicable law. We cannot approve a refund on Apple's behalf or promise its processing time. Contact us for app or purchase-identification help and Apple for its billing decision.

Google Play purchases

For a purchase billed by Google Play, use Google's refund guide and request process with the Google account used for the transaction. Eligibility and the available route depend on the purchase and regional rules.

You can also email breaksonfridays@gmail.com about a Google Play purchase, including when Google directs you to the developer. Include the relevant order reference and describe the problem. We review developer-handled requests using store tools and applicable law. Approval and payment-provider processing times are not guaranteed.

Pro has not unlocked

A pending purchase unlocks Pro only after store confirmation. If payment completed but Pro is missing, check your connection and store account, then use Restore Purchases in Settings or on the Pro screen. Do not repeatedly buy the product to try to fix access.

If access still does not return, email the platform, approximate purchase date, relevant transaction or order reference and error message. Redact unrelated information. Never send passwords, authentication codes or full card details.

Restoring and changing devices

Restore Purchases checks ownership with the same store account. App Store purchases do not automatically transfer to Google Play, and Google Play purchases do not automatically transfer to the App Store. Store eligibility and availability still apply.

Restoring Pro restores access, not local bills, payments, savings or game history. Use Backup & Restore or an available device backup for those records. A local reset or uninstall does not itself refund or remove purchase ownership.

After a refund or reversal

A confirmed refund, reversal or revocation may remove Pro when entitlement is next verified. Existing financial records are not erased. Free-tier creation limits may apply to new records while existing records remain available to manage.

A refund does not erase local records, exported files or transaction records the store must retain. Use Delete local data for the installation and the Data & Deletion instructions for other copies.

Your statutory rights

This policy does not limit rights in your country, including remedies for faulty or misdescribed digital content and applicable withdrawal or refund rights. Store processes do not replace protections required by law. Contact the publisher if a purchase issue remains unresolved.
